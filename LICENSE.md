# Rights and Licenses for the American Standard Version Bible

The files in this distribution combine material with different legal statuses. No single copyright claim or license applies to every field.

## Public-domain text

The American Standard Version (1901) text is public domain. The Westminster Leningrad Codex biblical text is public domain. Their inclusion in generated USX does not place them under a new copyright or license.

The files under `usx-english-only/` contain the ASV text without alignment markup. The aligned files under `usx-english-aligned/` and `usx-original-aligned/` additionally contain the source data and annotations described below.
